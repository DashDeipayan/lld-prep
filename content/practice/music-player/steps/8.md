Answer each follow-up in 2–4 lines, naming **which classes change**:

1. *"Add crossfade: the next song fades in over the last 5 seconds."*
2. *"Free tier: play an ad after every 3 songs; ads can't be skipped."*
3. *"Add 'Play next' and 'Add to queue'."*
4. *"Support offline downloads."*

<!-- answer -->

## 1. Crossfade

- The `AudioOutput` contract grows a `preload(nextSong)` and a `crossfade(durationMs)`, or a `CrossfadeOutput` **decorator** wraps an existing output and runs two decoders.
- The player asks the queue for a **peek** of the next song (`queue.peekNext(repeat)`) without moving the cursor, then commits on `trackEnded`.
- The state machine is unchanged, and the queue gains one read-only method.

## 2. Ads on the free tier

- A new **state** `AdPlaying`. In it, `next`/`previous`/`pause`-to-skip are no-ops, and `trackEnded` returns to `Playing` with the *queued* song.
- An `AdPolicy` **strategy** (`shouldPlayAd(songsSinceLastAd, tier)`) is consulted on each automatic or manual advance. Premium gets a `NoAdsPolicy`.
- That's a new state and a new strategy. Existing states change only where they hand off to `AdPlaying`.

## 3. Play next / add to queue

- `PlayQueue.playNext(song)` appends to `songs` and inserts that index into `order` at `pos + 1`. `addToQueue(song)` appends to the user-queue section.
- Spotify keeps a separate **user queue** that takes priority over the context queue. Model it as two lists, and have `next()` drain the user queue first. Un-shuffle only touches the context list.

## 4. Offline downloads

- A `DownloadManager` with `DownloadTask` states (`QUEUED → DOWNLOADING → DONE / FAILED`, retry), and storage limits.
- A **Strategy** to pick the source: `SongSource { open(song) }` with `LocalFileSource` and `StreamingSource`. A `CachingSource` decorator prefers local and falls back to streaming.
- The player and queue don't change. The audio output just asks the source for a stream.

> **Close with:** *"Most follow-ups landed as a new state, a new listener or a new adapter. The queue and the player context barely moved."*
