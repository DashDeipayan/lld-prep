## Questions worth asking

- Is this the **client-side player** (LLD), or the streaming service (system design)? *(the player and the user's library, in one process)*
- What's in the library? Songs, albums, artists, playlists? *(yes, and user-created playlists)*
- What can the user do? *(play, pause, stop, next, previous, seek, shuffle, repeat, add to queue)*
- Repeat modes? *(off, repeat-all, repeat-one)*
- What does "previous" do mid-song? *(restart the song if more than 3s in, otherwise go to the previous track)*
- If shuffle is turned off, where do we resume? *(at the same song, in original order)*
- Who reacts to playback? *(now-playing UI, lock screen, scrobbling/history)*
- Output devices? Can the user pick one, and switch mid-song? *(yes: phone speaker, wired headphones, Bluetooth, Chromecast/AirPlay. Switching keeps the song, position and play/pause state.)*
- What if the active device disconnects? *(fall back to the phone speaker and **pause**, so audio never suddenly blasts out of the phone)*
- Single user? Concurrency? *(one user, but audio callbacks come from another thread)*

## Agreed functional requirements

1. A **library** of songs (title, artist, album, duration), and user **playlists** (ordered, songs can repeat).
2. Playing a playlist/album loads a **play queue** and starts at a chosen song.
3. Controls: **play, pause, stop, next, previous**. Previous restarts the song if more than 3 seconds in.
4. **Shuffle** on/off without losing the current song. Turning it off returns to the original order.
5. **Repeat** OFF / ONE / ALL. With OFF, playback stops at the end of the queue.
6. When a song ends, the player **advances automatically** according to repeat/shuffle.
7. **Listeners** are told when the song or the play state changes.
8. **Audio device selection**: list the connected output devices, and let the user switch at any time. The song continues on the new device from the same second, playing stays playing and paused stays paused. Devices can appear and disappear at runtime. If the active one disconnects, switch to the phone speaker and pause.

## Non-functional

- The player is consistent even when "track ended" (audio thread) and button presses (UI thread) interleave.
- Easy to add new output types (each comes with its own vendor SDK) and new control surfaces (keyboard, headset button, voice).
- A device switch should feel instant: no restart from 0:00, no skipped song.

## Out of scope

Streaming/buffering protocols, DRM, recommendations, search ranking, payments, social features.
