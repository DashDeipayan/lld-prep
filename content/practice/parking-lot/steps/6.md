Ten gates run concurrently.

1. Describe **one concrete race condition** in your `park()` flow.
2. Propose a **fix**, and one **alternative** with its trade-off.
3. List **at least four edge cases** and how your design handles each.

<!-- answer -->

## The race

`park()` is **check-then-act**: *find a free spot*, then *occupy it*. Gates A and B both scan, both see `F1-M1` free, both occupy it, and two cars end up in one spot. The same applies to `platesInside`: two simultaneous parks of the same plate can both pass the duplicate check.

## Fixes

| Option | How | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **One lock on the lot** *(recommended in the interview)* | `synchronized`/`Mutex` around the body of park/unpark | Simple and obviously correct. Throughput is bounded, but each critical section takes microseconds while a car takes seconds at the gate, so it's fine. |
| Lock per floor | Try floors in order, lock one at a time | More parallelism, but "nearest" now needs care across floors, and the duplicate-plate set still needs its own lock |
| CAS per spot | `spot.tryOccupy(v)` with `compareAndSet(null, v)`, retry on failure | Lock-free, but the multi-step invariants (ticket map, plates set) need their own concurrent structures and the code gets subtle |

**Notify listeners outside the lock.** A slow display board must not stall every gate, and a listener that calls back into the lot would deadlock.

## Edge cases

- **Lot full** for that vehicle type: return a clear error. A bike can still park when only small spots are left.
- **Same plate parked twice**: rejected by `platesInside`.
- **Ticket used twice / unknown ticket**: `active.remove(id)` returns null → error. Never charge twice.
- **Lost ticket**: look up by plate (add a `plate → ticketId` map) and charge a lost-ticket fee. That's a new pricing rule, so it goes in the strategy.
- **Exactly 60 minutes**: decide whether that's 1 or 2 hours. "Started hour" means 60:00 → 1 hour and 60:01 → 2 hours. Write a test for it.
- **Clock goes backwards** (NTP): clamp the duration at zero. Injecting `Clock` makes this testable.
- **Spot out of service**: an `enabled` flag on `Spot`, and the free filter skips disabled spots.
