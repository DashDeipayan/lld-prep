The interviewer said: *"Design a risk management system for our payments platform. Every transaction should be checked before we authorise it."*

Write down:
1. The **questions** you'd ask (aim for 6–10).
2. The **functional requirements** you'd agree on, including example rules.
3. **Non-functional** requirements (latency, failure behaviour, audit).
4. **Out of scope**.

## Questions worth asking

- Which risk? Payments fraud, trading position limits, credit risk? *(payment transactions: fraud and abuse)*
- Where does it sit? *(synchronously in the payment flow, before authorisation)*
- What does it return? *(a decision **ALLOW / REVIEW / BLOCK**, plus the reasons)*
- What happens on REVIEW? *(the payment is held, and a case goes to a manual review queue)*
- Example rules? *(amount over a limit, too many transactions in a window, blocklisted user/device/IP, new device, country differs from home country)*
- Is every rule a hard block, or do they add up? *(both: some block outright, others add to a risk score)*
- Who changes rules and how often? *(risk analysts, several times a week, without a deploy)*
- Latency budget? *(p99 < 50 ms for the whole check)*
- If a rule errors, allow or block? *(don't silently allow: send to review)*
- Audit requirements? *(every decision, with every rule hit, must be stored)*

## Functional requirements

1. `assess(transaction) → Assessment(decision, score, hits[])`.
2. Rules are **pluggable**: amount limit, velocity (count/amount per user per window), blocklist (user/device/IP), new device, country mismatch.
3. A rule can **hard-block**, or contribute a **score**. The total score maps to a decision through **thresholds** (e.g. ≥ 50 REVIEW, ≥ 80 BLOCK).
4. Rules and thresholds can be **changed at runtime**.
5. Every assessment is **audited**. REVIEW decisions create a **review case**.

## Non-functional

- p99 latency under 50 ms, and many concurrent transactions.
- **Correct velocity counts under concurrency.** Two simultaneous payments can't both slip under the limit.
- A broken rule must not take the system down or silently allow everything.
- Decisions are **explainable** (reasons) and **reproducible** (know which rule config was used).

## Out of scope

ML model training, the case-management UI, chargebacks, KYC onboarding, the payment authorisation itself.
